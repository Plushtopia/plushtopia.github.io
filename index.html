Plushtopia

Plushtopia is an upcoming 2D platformer MMORPG! Explore with friends in a vibrant world, take on quests, explore dungeons and find secrets..?

Characters

The inhabitants of Plushtopia are a variety of plushies! Choose from creatures such as pillbugs or dragons and customise as you wish! From purple and green to yellow and blue, choose whatever primary and secondary colour you want!

Features

  • Meet a variety of NPCs and take on quests
  • Explore a vast 2D map
  • Play alone or online with others
  • Collect equipment that changes your movement style
  • Delve into dungeons
...and more!

Contact

Currently, Plushtopia news is being posted on X / Twitter, Bluesky and Tumblr. Feel free to reach out on any of these platforms although responses may be slow. Otherwise, more contact details will be added in the future, so bookmark this page for future updates!

Development

Plushtopia is currently being developed as a solo project and has been in active development since the start of 2025. No generative AI is used in all aspects of the game.
